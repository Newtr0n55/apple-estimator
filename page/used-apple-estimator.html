    Assumptions behind the percentages

    What comes from the listings: the base price of each model, read from the clean listings, and the bypass discount. Bypassed units were priced about 20 to 55% under comparable ones. The chip under the model says how many listings the base price rests on.

    What is my assumption: every other percentage. The listings give no reliable signal for Face ID faults, battery wear or cracked glass, because sellers price these inconsistently. Change any number and the receipt updates. Changes are kept in this browser.
